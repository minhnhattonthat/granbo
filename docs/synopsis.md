# Synopsis (who is who; grows as batches report)

- **The player** ({9400}): a 10-year-old, grandchild of Genzo. Gets a first Granbo on their birthday and becomes a
  Granbo Saver. Default name from name entry.
- **Old Gen / Genzo** (ゲンじい / ゲンゾウ): the player's grandfather, a legendary retired Granbo Saver. Gruff, proud,
  funny. Gives the player the Data Pad and Hunt Glove.
- **Shizuku** (シズク): a polite girl who crash-lands near the house riding an unknown, badly damaged Granbo named
  **Grisha** (グリーシャ). She refuses help at first to keep the family out of danger; the Sky Shark gang is after her.
- **Akari** (アカリ, energetic girl, says アタシ) and **Daigoro** (ダイゴロウ, big easygoing boy, ～なんだな): the
  player's friends and rivals.
- **Sky Shark** (スカイシャーク): villain gang aiming at world conquest with the towers' "Grand Change". Bosses named
  after seas: **Baltic** (バルト), **Aegean** (エーゲ), **Bermuda** (バミューダ, coldly polite). **Grunts** (子分).
  After their defeat they try to become world champions "another way": Granbo battles at the Battle Stadium.
- **Grand Change Towers** change a region's land; the **World Change Tower** appears late and collapses at the end.
- **Granbo Saver ranks**: Chick, Rookie, Amateur, Pro, Three-Star, Veteran, Master, Super, Ultra, King, God.
- Post-game: new tournaments at the Battle Stadium and **Secret Dungeons** appear on each continent.
